Patch 5/5: b43: N-PHY: add the missing override to the rev7 TX_PU command

TX_PU override in the PAPD cal setup. Override bank 2 (REV7_RF_CTL_OVER5,
0x346, and its value register MISC_REG5, 0x344): wl sets field 0x1 and
then field 0x4 (0x1 -> 0x5). papd8 (b43 with the fix) does the same.
Stock b43 never runs PAPD, so there is no trace of it without the fix.
