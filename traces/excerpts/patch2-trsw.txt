Patch 2/5: b43: N-PHY: fix TX IQ/LO calibration

TRSW override for the second core (RFCTL_INTC2, field 7:6) during the cal setup.
wl sets the field to 2 (0x1000 -> 0x1080) and then the enable bit (0x1480).
b43 without the fix writes 8 << 6 = 0x200 (bit 9, outside the field); the
register reads back 0x1000, so only the enable bit is left (0x1400).
papd8 (b43 with the fix) matches wl.

== raw/wl.trace
   55.796751 PHY   R 092       RFCTL_INTC2                  0x1000
   55.796756 PHY   W 092       RFCTL_INTC2                  0x1080
   55.796763 PHY   R 092       RFCTL_INTC2                  0x1080
   55.796765 PHY   W 092       RFCTL_INTC2                  0x1480
