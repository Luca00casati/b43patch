Patch 2/5: b43: N-PHY: fix TX IQ/LO calibration

Busy poll on IQLOCAL_CMD: bits 15:14 stay set while a step runs.
wl writes 0x8434, then reads it back with bits 15:14 still set until the step ends.
b43 without the fix reads it once and goes straight to the result buffer
(table 15 at 0x60), which is still empty.
